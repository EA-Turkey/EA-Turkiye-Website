## About the Summit

The Effective Altruism Istanbul Summit 2026 will take place on October 24, 2026, at HAN Spaces Etiler in Istanbul.

Effective altruism is a philosophy and social movement focused on finding the most effective ways to solve the world’s biggest problems—such as global poverty, animal welfare, and catastrophic risks from artificial intelligence. This event will bring together members of Effective Altruism Turkey and is aimed at both individuals who have been engaging with EA principles for a long time, and those who are just beginning to explore how they can make a greater impact through their careers, time, and donations.

Through talks, discussions, and networking opportunities, the event aims to spark new ideas, deepen engagement, and strengthen the effective altruism community in Turkey.

The event will be mostly conducted in Turkish.

## Is there an event fee?

Our event is free of charge.

### Who is the event for?

- Highly motivated students, professionals, and individuals considering a career change who want to make a meaningful impact in the world and are eager to learn how to do so more effectively
- People who enjoy thinking about pressing global issues such as global poverty, animal welfare, artificial intelligence, and other existential risks
- Individuals of all ages and experience levels who want to learn more about effective altruism
- Long-time EA enthusiasts who want to engage with advanced topics, meet collaborators, and dive into specific cause areas
- Members of Effective Altruism Turkey

### What to expect at the event

- Introductory talks covering the core concepts and focus areas of effective altruism
- Intermediate-level sessions on the world’s most pressing problems and potential solutions
- Office hours and an organization fair showcasing EA-aligned opportunities
- Skill-building workshops and advanced interactive sessions to go deeper
- Meetups and social events where you can connect with peers, mentors, and potential collaborators
